Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where does the forecast data come from?
Surfcast pulls from established global marine models — ECMWF (WAM), NOAA GFS Wave, Météo-France MFWAM and DWD GWAM — plus tide data, so you're reading the same science the pros rely on.
Which regions are covered?
Coastlines worldwide. Search by country, region or spot name, browse breaks near you, or explore the live map to find anywhere with rideable swell.
Is there a home-screen widget?
Yes. Pin any spot to a widget and see live wave height, period, wind and the full day's tide curve without opening the app.
Do I need an account?
No sign-up to start checking forecasts. Download, search your spot and go — your favourites and settings stay on your device.
Is Surfcast on Android?
Surfcast is on iPhone and iPad today, with Android on the way. Follow the blog for release news.
